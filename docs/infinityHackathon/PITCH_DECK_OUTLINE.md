## Slide 5: How It Works 🔧

### Technical Architecture (Simplified)

```
1️⃣ Kreator membuat karya (draft, desain, musik, dll)
    ↓
2️⃣ Upload ke VaultNotes dengan enkripsi client-side
    ↓
3️⃣ Blockchain timestamp otomatis (immutable proof)
    ↓
4️⃣ Disimpan terdesentralisasi di ICP network
    ↓
5️⃣ Kreator bisa share dengan permission control
    ↓
6️⃣ Setiap edit tercatat di audit trail
    ↓
7️⃣ Export legal certificate kapan pun dibutuhkan
```

**Tech Stack:**
- **Frontend:** React 19 + Vite + TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Rust WebAssembly on ICP
- **Encryption:** vetKD threshold cryptography
- **Auth:** Internet Identity (Web3 login)
- **AI:** On-chain content summarization & fingerprinting

## Slide 6: Key Features 🌟

### What Makes VaultNotes Different?

#### 1. **Immutable Timestamping**
- Setiap karya dicap waktu di ICP blockchain
- Tidak bisa diubah/dihapus retroaktively
- Bukti legal: "Saya buat ini di tanggal X jam Y"

#### 2. **End-to-End Encryption**
- vetKD protocol = threshold cryptography
- Hanya kreator yang punya akses
- Bahkan VaultNotes tidak bisa baca konten Anda

#### 3. **Smart Sharing & Permissions**
- Read-only vs Edit access
- Audit trail setiap perubahan
- Revoke access kapan saja

#### 4. **AI Content Fingerprinting**
- Deteksi similarity untuk anti-plagiarisme
- Auto-summarization untuk quick reference
- Performance metrics & quality scoring

#### 5. **Decentralized Storage**
- Tidak ada single point of failure
- Data Anda tetap ada selamanya
- Censorship-resistant

| **Speed** | ✅ 1-2 sec finality | ❌ 15+ sec | ⚠️ 2-3 sec |
| **Storage** | ✅ On-chain unlimited | ❌ Extremely expensive | ❌ Expensive |
| **Web3 Auth** | ✅ Internet Identity | ⚠️ Metamask friction | ⚠️ Metamask friction |

- **Rust Backend** → WebAssembly for performance
- **vetKD Protocol** → No single private key compromise
