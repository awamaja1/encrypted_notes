## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Tell a story:** Problem → Solution → Proof → Future
2. **Show, don't tell:** Screenshots > descriptions
3. **Use data:** Numbers build credibility
4. **Be visual:** Images > text walls
5. **Practice pitch:** Rehearse out loud 3-5 times
6. **Time yourself:** Should be 10-15 min presentation
7. **Anticipate questions:** Prepare answers for common objections
8. **Exude confidence:** You have a great product!
